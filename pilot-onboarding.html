How Sunlytics works

Every time a homeowner orders a drone report on sunlytics.fi, a mission is created and sent to pilots in that area. Here is the full flow from notification to payment.

1

You get an SMS and email

"New mission in Espoo. Address: Mannerheimintie 45. Reward: €40. Accept?" You have 30 minutes to claim it before it goes to the next pilot.

2

Accept in the pilot dashboard

Log in to your pilot dashboard, hit Accept, and the mission is locked to you. The homeowner is notified automatically.

3

Fly the roof

Go to the address and fly a standard grid pattern over the roof. We recommend 30m altitude, 80% overlap, nadir angle. The whole flight takes 10 to 20 minutes.

4

Upload your images

Go to upload-mission.html from your dashboard and upload the images before your mission deadline. Minimum 40 images per standard roof.

5

Get paid automatically

Once images are validated by our system (usually within a few hours), your payment is released to your bank or Revolut. No invoicing needed.

Important: only fly when it is safe

Do not fly in wind above 10 m/s, rain, or temperatures below 0°C. Our system checks weather before scheduling but always use your own judgment. If conditions are unsafe, contact us and we reschedule at no penalty to you.

Flying your first mission

Here is exactly what to do from the moment you get a mission notification to the moment you upload your images.

1

Check the mission details

Address, roof type, and any notes from our team. Google Street View the address before you go so you know what to expect.

2

Check airspace and NOTAMs

Use droneinfo.fi (Traficom's official drone map) to confirm the address is in uncontrolled airspace. If it is within 1km of an airport, contact us before flying.

3

Flight settings

Altitude: 30 to 40m above roof level. Overlap: 80% front, 70% side. Camera angle: 90° nadir (straight down). Image format: JPEG at full resolution. Minimum 40 images for a standard house, 80 for a warehouse.

4

Cover the full roof

Fly a grid pattern covering the entire roof plus 5m margin on all sides. Do not miss any sections. Partial coverage is the most common reason for image rejection.

5

Upload within 24 hours

Go to your pilot dashboard, open the mission, and use the Upload Images button. Images must be uploaded within 24 hours of the flight. After that the mission may be reassigned.

Common rejection reasons

Blurry images (fly slower), incomplete coverage, images taken at wrong angle (check your gimbal is set to nadir), or too few images. If your images are rejected you will be notified and given a chance to reshoot within 48 hours.
