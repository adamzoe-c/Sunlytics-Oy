1. Our Services

Sunlytics provides the following services:

  • Free preliminary analysis: a free, instant solar estimate based on satellite imagery, available for residential and commercial properties
  • Professional drone survey: an on-site commercial property survey performed by an authorized drone pilot, with a PDF report delivered after the flight. Pricing depends on the size, location, and scope of the property and is confirmed with you before booking

2. Accuracy of Free Analysis

The free preliminary analysis is an estimate based on satellite data and solar irradiance models. Results may vary by 10–20% from actual conditions. This estimate is provided for informational purposes only and should not be used as the sole basis for investment decisions. For full accuracy, we recommend ordering a professional drone survey.

3. Drone Survey Service

Professional drone surveys are carried out by authorized, appropriately licensed drone pilots operating in accordance with applicable civil aviation regulations. By ordering this service:

  • You confirm that you are the property owner, or an authorized representative of the property owner, with the right to authorize drone access to the property (see Section 4)
  • If ordering on behalf of a business or organization, you confirm that you have the authority to bind that entity to these terms
  • You agree that drone flights are subject to weather conditions, airspace restrictions, and site access, and may be rescheduled accordingly
  • You understand that the report is delivered within 24 hours of successful completion of the drone flight

4. Property Access and Owner Consent

Before a drone survey is scheduled, you must confirm that you are the legal owner of the property to be surveyed, or that you are an authorized representative acting with the consent of the property owner — for example, a tenant, facilities manager, procurement contact, or contractor with documented authorization. Sunlytics may request written confirmation of this authorization before scheduling or conducting a survey. It is your responsibility to notify any relevant on-site personnel, tenants, or occupants of neighboring properties of the scheduled drone flight where required by law or site policy.

5. Survey and Report Data

During a drone survey, Sunlytics collects imagery, measurements, and other technical data about the property ("Survey Data"). Survey Data is used to produce your report and may be retained by Sunlytics for quality assurance, service improvement, and record-keeping. The finished report and Survey Data are provided to you for your own use, including for evaluating solar installation options, but you do not receive any right to resell the report or Survey Data as a standalone product. Sunlytics retains ownership of the underlying Survey Data, analysis tools, and methodology used to produce your report, in accordance with Section 10 (Intellectual Property).

6. Authorized Installer Access

If you engage a solar installer participating in the Sunlytics installer program, you may authorize Sunlytics to share your report and Survey Data directly with that installer. Installers accessing your report and Survey Data through Sunlytics may only use it to prepare quotes or installation plans for you and must not use, store, or share it for any other purpose. You may withdraw this authorization at any time by contacting us at info@sunlytics.fi.

7. Payment

Payment for a professional drone survey is due before the survey takes place, unless otherwise agreed in writing, for example under a separate invoicing arrangement for commercial or installer-partner accounts. We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and bank transfer. All prices are in Euros and include VAT where applicable. Pricing for drone surveys depends on the size, location, and scope of the property and is confirmed with you before booking.

8. Cancellations and Refunds

You may cancel your drone survey order up to 24 hours before the scheduled survey for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the survey may be subject to a cancellation fee. If a drone flight cannot be completed due to weather after three attempts, a full refund will be issued.

8a. EU Right of Withdrawal (14-Day Cooling-Off Period)

Under the Finnish Consumer Protection Act (Kuluttajansuojalaki) and EU Directive 2011/83/EU, if you are a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 calendar days of placing your order, without giving any reason.

To exercise this right, notify us clearly before the 14-day period expires by emailing info@sunlytics.fi with your name, order reference, and a statement that you wish to withdraw. We will confirm receipt and process your refund within 14 days using the same payment method you used.

Important exception: if you expressly request that the drone survey begins before the 14-day withdrawal period expires, and you confirm that you understand you will lose your right of withdrawal once the service is fully performed, the right of withdrawal no longer applies once the flight has been completed and the report delivered.

Business customers: this right of withdrawal applies only where you are acting as a consumer — that is, a natural person acting for purposes outside your trade, business, craft, or profession. If you are ordering a survey on behalf of a company or other commercial entity, this statutory withdrawal right does not apply to your order, and cancellations are instead governed by Section 8 (Cancellations and Refunds) above.
